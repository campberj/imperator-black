# Imperator Black: closed alpha

**Rules v0.5 · solo 28mm sci-fi skirmish · private playtest, not for distribution**

Thank you for testing Imperator Black. It is a military science fiction skirmish game for 28mm collections. A small team of named soldiers boards ships and stations, or fights across planetside terrain, against an enemy run by the game itself. It is built to be played **solo**, and it plays like a firefight rather than a duel: weapons reach across the table, so cover, suppression and fire support decide more than who charges first.

This is an **alpha**. The rules have been played, but not by many people and not in every situation. Some rules are marked **Proposed**: they are new and untested. Your games are how we find out what works.

## What you need

- **Dice:** two six-sided dice in one colour (the hit dice), and a pair each of d4, d6, d8, d10 and d12 in another colour (the damage dice). A few extra d6 help.
- **Miniatures:** 3 to 8 models for your side, and around 6 to 15 for the enemy. Any 28mm sci-fi models will do. The **Generic forces** profiles fit most line infantry, heavy weapons, storm troopers and power-armoured heavy infantry.
- **Terrain:** for ship and station fights, a few square feet of corridor and room tiles (6"×6", 6"×3" and 3"×3" work best) with walls and doors. For planetside fights, a 3'×3' table with cover.
- **Tokens:** coloured cubes or counters in six colours (see the token table in the quick reference), numbered chips (poker chips work) for hidden enemy contacts, and a tape measure in inches.
- **Printouts:** the quick reference, the unit and behavior cards for the models you use, and a mission dashboard (all in the appendices). A dry-erase sleeve for the dashboard is handy.

## How to start

1. Read chapters 1 and 2 (core concepts, the round, actions and reactions), then skim chapters 3 to 7. The quick reference covers most of what you need at the table.
2. **Play Gym 1: First Breach** (in the appendices). It is a short ship-boarding fight that teaches movement, shooting, doors and the solo opponent. Use the printed sheet, map and cards.
3. Then try **Gym 2** (land) or **Gym 3** (a station), or one of the two sample missions. With a second player, try a **Hold the ground** patrol game (chapter 16) first: it has no hidden information.
4. When you're comfortable, build your own: generate a deck (chapter 11) or a land table (chapter 12), and a mission (chapter 13).

## Running the enemy

The enemy is run by chapter 10, the solo opponent, and its behavior cards. Each enemy element has a behavior (Sentry, Tactical, Aggressive and so on). When an enemy model activates, read its card top-down and take the first line that applies. If you ever have to make a judgment call the rules don't settle, **note it**: that is one of the most useful things you can report.

Two players can play **co-operatively**, sharing the player force against the solo opponent, or **against each other** with chapter 16: forces matched by Troop Value (Appendix I), three even fights and three raids. The two-player rules are brand new and entirely Proposed: they are a large part of what this alpha tests.

## What we want to know

After each game, fill in the feedback form at the back (or just answer its questions in a message). The most useful reports say:

- which rules you had to look up, guess at, or invent at the table;
- where the enemy did something no real enemy would do, or needed a judgment call;
- anything that felt too lethal, too safe, too swingy or too slow;
- how long the game took, and how long setup took;
- which Proposed rules you used, and how they felt.

Please report what happened, not just what you think should change. One rule change needs the same problem to show up in more than one game.

## What isn't in this alpha

- Vehicles, walkers, and air or orbital support.
- The campaign and the troop builder. They exist in draft and will come in a later alpha. (Each troop's Troop Value is included, in Appendix I, for two-player games.)

## A note on the forces

Imperator Black is model-agnostic: any miniature can be given a profile in minutes (chapter 9). Besides the Generic forces, this alpha includes **fan conversion profiles** for miniatures some testers own: BLKOUT (Task Force Boone, Manticor Borz, Harlow, UN Force Command), Star Wars: Legion (the Galactic Republic) and Near Present (Anchorage and Hankou). These names and settings belong to their owners. The profiles are unofficial, are shared privately and without charge for this playtest, and must not be distributed further.
