# Proposal: PoC Opposition Force

**Status:** Proposed (2026-10-20). Awaiting RJ's call.

## The need

The proof of concept is solo boarding actions against Task Force Boone, whose Void Assault Teams are trained for boarding and zero-g combat. The opposition must:

1. Make sense aboard ships and stations.
2. Support all four PoC mission types: Breach and Clear, Recover Personnel, Seize Data/Cargo, Sabotage.
3. Span Q0 to Q2 so the generator can scale threat, and so playtesting exercises the whole quality range.
4. Absorb a mixed collection of BLKOUT and BPRE miniatures under one banner (pillar 2).
5. Leave room for a harder, peer opponent later in the campaign.

## Options

| | Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Void corsairs** — a privateer and smuggling coalition that seizes ships and stations, hires mercenaries and holds captive crews | Natural boarding-action foe; hostages, cargo, data and sabotage all fit; widest quality range under one banner; mixed models look right together | Less of a "real war" feel than a state military |
| B | Rival naval infantry — a peer state's marines defending its own ships | Hardest, most doctrinal fights; mirrors your force | Narrow quality range (all Q1–Q2); fewer mission types make sense; little variety for the generator |
| C | Corporate security holding a station | Good for station raids and data theft | Weak fit for ship actions; thin variety |

**Recommendation: A for the PoC, with B as the escalation foe for a later Black Ops campaign.** Corsairs give the generator and behavior tables the most to work with. A peer military arriving mid-campaign is also a strong narrative turn once the core is proven.

The faction name is a placeholder until you pick one.

## Two mechanics this enables

### Contact tokens

Your numbered poker chips start as **unknown contacts**, not known enemies (Zone Raiders–style, adopted in the AWoW teardown).

- A contact is revealed when a player model gains line of sight to it, or when noise occurs within a set distance.
- On reveal, roll on the mission's **contact table**. The app prints it, tuned to the mission's threat level. Results run from *false alarm* through *crew*, *guard pair*, *enforcer team* and *specialist*, to *booby trap*.
- Rolling at reveal keeps solo play uncertain without needing a hidden key that you would have to avoid reading.
- Unrevealed contacts move under a simple rule: toward noise once the alert level allows it.

### Alert track (0–6)

A mission clock that rises with noise and escalates the response.

| Raises alert | |
|---|---|
| Shooting (not suppressed or silenced) | +1 |
| Breaching a door or bulkhead | +1 |
| Explosion | +2 |
| A revealed enemy leaves your line of sight alive, or a guard uses its Raise Alarm action | +1 |

| Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2 | Unrevealed contacts start moving toward the last noise |
| 4 | The contract boarding team arrives at a set entry point over the next rounds |
| 6 | Lockdown: a mission-specific event (doors seal, hostages are moved, data is wiped, the ship tries to break dock) |

This rewards Black Ops play (quiet entry, fast action) and gives mission timing real weight. It also gives the after-action report a campaign consequence: a high alert level becomes intelligence the enemy keeps.

### Surrender

Cowed crew who surrender become **prisoners**. Prisoners are a campaign resource (intelligence, ransom, reputation) and a tactical burden (they need a guard to move). This feeds pillar 7.
