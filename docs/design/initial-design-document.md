# Imperator Black: A Science Fiction Wargame System

*Initial design document, converted from the original Word file.*

**Mission Statement:** Imperator Black is a narrative wargame system with a broad scope, covering small unit boarding actions and skirmishes, to abstracted combat over star systems in a fictional universe.

## Inspirations

### Wargames

**Five Parsecs from Home** – This game system allows for heavy narrative to develop through its table driven random events, quests, contracts, and items. In addition, allowing for crew growth through experience and traits, along with new and unique items has the player invested in their game.

**Five Parsecs: Tactics** – This game system is similar to Five Parsecs from Home, but is more aligned with military actions, and allows for large battles with squads and the inclusion of vehicles. With the field commanders guide, this ruleset allows for a variety of campaigns to be played.

**BLKOUT** – BLKOUT is unique in that while the rules are not particularly long, the way that teams activate, and the ready/reaction mechanics allow for fluid and reactive gameplay, and allow for realistic small unit tactics to be employed.

**Space Weirdos** – This game I have not personally played. However, I am drawn to the idea of using dice pairs of varying sizes, ie 2d4, 2d6, etc. as this allows for expected averages, and allows for granularity of results.

**Black Powder Red Earth 28mm** – Designed by an experienced special operations training advisor, this ruleset correctly allows for weapons to have unlimited range, and uses a 2d10 system to allow for players to correctly know the odds based on their units. Terminology is accurate for modern/near future military, and it correctly models assets available to modern and near future militaries such as electronic warfare, drone warfare, and organic weapon systems such as crew served weapons.

### Novels

**The Expanse** – This novel series is near and dear to me. The characters are deep, the setting is believable due to being grounded in accurate understandings of physics, science, and mathematics.

**The Galaxy's Edge Series, by Nick Cole and Jason Anspach** – The authors are US military veterans, and it shows. The combat is realistic, the terminology and slang are correct, and the story draws you in.

**The Forever War** – I have only begun to read this, but to my understanding it is a seminal read for realistic science fiction.

## Game Overview

The game will be split into three components or layers; the tabletop/tactical level, the region of space/territory level, and planet/system campaign level. While the game will have rules, tables, etc that allow for a player to play with only physical components and printed rules, a web based application will be created that supports the three game layers, and allows for a large number of people to participate in shared campaigns. The vision for this is that a number of players would join a shared narrative campaign, and each campaign turn either command or conduct missions they have been tasked with, and report and log the results after each battle. This in turn will link into the shared campaign at large. The details for each are below:

### The Tabletop

The table top level of Imperator Black is where the rubber meets the road, so to speak. This is the most important layer of the game, and where a player should be looking forward to engaging. This will be a 28mm ruleset, with players controlling a small team of individual models, with alternating activations. Initial scope of play is for small teams, but will eventually grow to platoon level or larger engagements. A lore or setting point, likely the ubiquity of autonomous guided munitions, or simple difficulty in moving larger numbers, will explain why conflicts are not often fought at larger levels. Games will be conducted both in void/space combat within ships, space stations, etc. As well as planetside. The game will feature an organic AI system, allowing for solo play, along with cooperative play. Rules shall also be developed so as to allow for player vs player adversarial campaigns and missions to be fought. The application will provide the layout for the mission, the mission/tasking details/objectives, the atmospherics, and ship or battlefield layout, along with enemies and any assets available.

### The Ship/Outpost Level

This is where the players are based, and determines what missions, taskings, etc. they receive. This should be designed in a way so that players can upgrade systems and gain both campaign level benefits, as well as assets, etc. available during missions. The application will manage all aspects of the crew outside of combat here, along with travel, narrative events, communication with command, etc.

### The Sector/Campaign Level

This is the map or node based level, where players will traverse to different areas depending on the campaign and mission requirements. The application will handle this, and in single player campaigns act as commander for the player and their forces, and in multiplayer campaigns should allow for players to communicate, plot out courses, and send forces to fight for control of different bases, planets, etc.
