## What happened

The team took the cargo hold first: the assaulter cracked D1 and threw a flashbang, which won the fight for the hold. Task Force Boone then defeated the enemy in detail. The sentry gunner investigated the noise and fought a multi-round firefight with the Pathfinder. The team went the long way round instead of hacking D3; the sentry gunner opened the locked door from its side. The officer in the hold went Down and was captured when it tried to get up in base contact with a VAT assaulter.

## Rules friction

- **Reaction timing** (round 2): VAT gunner shoots the sentry gunner, who returns fire. Who lands first? Settled: the trigger resolves in full, then reactions (chapter 2, Timing, with a worked example).
- **Wounded and recovery:** is Wounded −1 per marker or flat; does it affect recovery; what happens to markers on recovery? Clarified in chapter 7. Recovery was the only rule looked up during play.
- **Breaking test, recovery next to enemies:** clarified (breaking every activation, enemy elements count as fireteams, fled by the entry point); recovery under guard and capture added (decisions 4.16, 8.12).
- **Hit rate:** "it took a LOT of shots to get hits and effective results, especially considering both sides are trained and are engaging each other within 20 feet" (about 3" on the table). Raised twice in this game. See decisions 3.19 and 3.20 and the [hit target comparison](../analysis/hit-target-comparison.md).

## AI behavior

- **Almost no judgment calls.** "The enemy is excellent now, especially with clear guidance on moving to cover, investigating, etc." Meets the Phase 3 exit (at most one judgment call per round).
- **Noise and investigation worked:** the sentry gunner investigated and fought a multi-round firefight with the Pathfinder. "AI felt very good."
- **Leader worked:** the captain rallied a suppressed sentry.
- **Close combat** lasted two exchanges, then a Pinned sentry broke off and fell back to cover, as its card says.

## Cards and reference

The behavior cards were used at the table and were "clear and concise". The token colours worked. Time pressure felt good.
