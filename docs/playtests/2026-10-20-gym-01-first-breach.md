| | |
|---|---|

| **Result** | Not recorded |

## What happened

The boarding element played close-range gunfights rather than close combat. The Data Specialist's hack on D3 failed several times, so the team breached instead. The VAT gunner went on Overwatch to cover a breach of a door by a VAT assaulter.

## Rules friction

- **Doors and reactions:** when a door opens it is unclear who can react, to what, and in what order. During play this raised the question of whether an Overwatch model may fire at enemies a friendly door-opening reveals; ruled yes for the rest of the game (exposure trigger, decision 4.11).
- **Automatic fire:** whether Auto weapons can engage several targets. Auto N was adopted mid-game (decision 4.12).
- **Hacking:** failed repeatedly at 9+. The variety of target numbers was enjoyable.

## AI behavior

- **Noise didn't change behaviour.** After the breach the enemy was aware, but models without line of sight simply went on Overwatch rather than responding to the noise.
- **Judgment calls in the cargo hold.** With VAT assaulters inside the hold with the guards, the enemy orders needed judgment calls. Not an interesting decision space, but realistic and understandable.

## Numbers check

- **Wounded markers** worked well. Enemy models were challenging to put down, which is good.
- **Close combat** was not attempted.

## Cards and reference

The cards and quick reference were great: the game was fully playable from the printed cards and quick reference. Room for refinement later.

## Changes to make

| Change | Rules section | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Write a clear door sequence: who may react when a door opens, to what, and in what order | Chapter 2, chapter 3 | High |
| Enemy response to noise and alert: investigate, reposition, reinforce, not just Overwatch | Phase 3 behavior tables | High |
| Enemy orders for fights inside the same compartment (target choice at close range, close in or fall back, grenades) | Phase 3 behavior tables | High |
| Review hacking: keep the target numbers; consider whether repeated failures need relief | Chapter 3 | Medium |
| Test close combat in a future gym game | Chapter 5 | Medium |
