# 8. Ship hazards

Hazards cover a **compartment** or a marked **area** (one or more 3"×3" grid cells). A mission, a complication, or an event during play (an explosion, a hull breach) can create them.

## Zero-g

In a zero-g area, nothing holds a model to the deck.

- **Push off:** a model moves in a **straight line** up to its Move. It must end in contact with a surface (wall, floor, terrain, door frame). If it doesn't, it keeps drifting: at the start of its next activation, it first moves the rest of its distance again in the same line, then acts.
- ***Void-trained*** models may stop at any surface they reach, and may change direction once per Move by pushing off a surface.
- **Unbraced:** a model not in contact with a surface takes −1 to Shoot, Throw and close combat, unless it is *Void-trained*.
- **Grenades** bounce rather than fall short (chapter 6).

## Decompression

A **hull breach** happens when a breaching charge or explosion opens a hull wall, or when a mission event says so.

1. The compartment **vents** at once. Every model in it tests 2d6 + Q (+2 if *Sealed*).
   - **8+:** it holds on.
   - **Fail:** it is dragged d6" straight toward the breach. A model that reaches the breach is **blown into space**: remove it. In the campaign it is missing in action.
2. Smoke and fire in the compartment are removed.
3. At the end of the round, the ship's emergency bulkheads **close and lock every door** into the compartment, unless the mission says the ship's systems are down.
4. The compartment is now in **vacuum**.

## Vacuum

- A model without *Sealed* armour that ends its activation in vacuum goes **Critical** (it dies at the end of the next round unless it is moved out of vacuum, or a Medic action gets 10+).
- Sound does not carry: shooting in vacuum does not count as noise.
- Fire cannot burn and smoke has no effect.

## Fire

Place a fire marker on a 3"×3" cell.

- A model that enters a fire cell, or ends its activation in one, takes **2d6** damage. *Sealed* armour counts as 1 point higher against fire.
- Fire and its smoke give **Light cover** to models in or behind the cell.
- **Spread:** at the end of each round, roll 2d6 for each fire cell. On 10+, fire spreads to one adjacent cell (not into vacuum). On 2–3, the fire burns out.
- **Extinguish:** a model with an extinguisher (or at an extinguisher point) in contact with the cell may Interact: test 8+ to remove the marker.

## Darkness

When a ship loses power, some or all areas go dark.

- A model in darkness can only be seen within **12"**, and shots beyond 6" take **−2** (*Low-light* models ignore this).
- A model that **shot this round** shows its muzzle flash: it can be seen at any distance in line of sight, and the darkness penalty to shoot at it is −1 instead of −2.

## Hazards and noise

Explosions are noise for the alert track; shooting in vacuum is not. Missions define any other effects.
