## Changes in v0.5

From the first gym playtest ([log](../docs/playtests/2026-10-20-gym-01-first-breach.md)) and Phase 3:

- **The door sequence** (chapter 3): who reacts when a door opens, and in what order. **Crack and throw**: open a door and throw a grenade through in one Interact.
- **Exposure trigger** (chapter 2): an Overwatch model may react to an enemy coming into view for any reason.
- **Auto N** (chapter 4): automatic weapons may engage several models in a 3" beaten zone.
- **Hacking security levels** (chapter 3): Basic to Military, some needing two successes; each failed attempt gives +1 to the next.
- **The solo opponent** (chapter 10): awareness and noise, the alarm, drill by training, fixed activation order and target choice, close quarters, surrender, and a priority list for every behavior profile. Replaces the stopgap enemy orders.
- **Land battles** (chapter 12): terrain types, height and falling, conditions, noise in the open, and land table setup for seven environments. The solo opponent's lists now work on land too.
- **Shoot scale** (chapter 1): what each Shoot value means; Task Force Boone line troopers are professionals (Shoot 2).
- **Ship and station layouts** (chapter 11): build a deck from your tiles with about 20 d6 rolls; four vessel types, rooms with cover, doors and hazards.
- **Missions** (chapter 13): four mission types, threat tiers 1–4, an open guard and hidden contact chips, the alert track, and a mission clock (a time limit and a clock die instead of a round limit). A one-page mission dashboard tracks it all.

## Changes in v0.4

Granular troop profiles, in the spirit of Infinity, on the same 2d6 engine:

- **Quality is replaced by attributes:** Move, Shoot, Fight, Physique, Nerve, Tech, Wounds and Size. Every test names the attribute it uses (chapter 1).
- **Rank** (Green to Elite) stays as a label; it never enters a roll.
- **Move varies by troop** (usually 5–7"), reflecting training, exoskeletons and heavy kit.
- **Wounds:** a model goes Down when it has more Wounded markers than its Wounds attribute.
- **Size:** 1 small drone, 2 human, 3 large. Crawlways and small hatches admit Size 2 or less.
- **Skills with levels** replace model keywords. First levelled skill: *Marksman* 1–2 (proposed).

Results: [sim-v0.4-results.md](../docs/analysis/sim-v0.4-results.md).

## Changes in v0.3

Lethality retune, from the [simulator findings](../docs/analysis/sim-v0.2-findings.md). Only content changed, plus the armour note in chapter 4:

- **Wound table re-banded:** suppression-only results now cover damage totals 2–5; Wounded starts at 6, Down at 10, Dead at 16.
- **Close-quarters weapons stepped up:** sidearm 2d6, SMG 2d8, carbine 2d8, shotgun 2d12.
- **Melee blades stepped up:** knife or bayonet 2d8, breaching tool or heavy blade 2d10 (unarmed stays 2d4).
- **Frag grenades** stepped up to 2d8.

Results: [sim-v0.3-results.md](../docs/analysis/sim-v0.3-results.md).

## Changes in v0.2 (from prototype v0.1)

- **Reactions:** unactivated models may react to enemy actions; Overwatch now allows up to two reactions until the model next activates.
- **Close combat:** opposed 2d6 + Q exchanges that continue until one side is incapacitated or both break away.
- **Grenades and demolitions:** frag, flashbang, smoke and breaching charges.
- **Ship interiors:** doors, locked doors, bulkheads and compartments.
- **Ship hazards:** zero-g, decompression, vacuum, fire and darkness.
- **Down recovery:** an end-of-round test fixes the row-6 gap.
- **Errata:** the Armour 3 note is corrected.
