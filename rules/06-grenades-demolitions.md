# 6. Grenades and demolitions

## Throw

**Throw** is an action. It is *Handy*: there is no penalty for throwing after a Move.

1. Choose a point the thrower can see, up to double the grenade's throw range. A point beyond an open door is allowed if the thrower can see through the doorway.
2. Test 2d6 + **Physique**, with −1 beyond the throw range.
3. On **8+** the grenade lands on the point.
4. On a miss, it lands **short**: move the point straight back toward the thrower by the amount the roll missed by (minimum 1"). If that path crosses a wall or a doorway edge, it stops there and drops.
5. On a **natural 2**, it slips: it lands 2" in front of the thrower.

**Crack and throw:** a model beside a closed, unlocked door may open it and throw a grenade through in one Interact, before anyone can react to the door opening (chapter 3).

In **zero-g**, a missed grenade bounces instead of falling short. Move the point straight past the target point by the miss distance, or until it hits a wall.

Each model carries **two grenades** unless its roster says otherwise. Choose the types before the mission and track them on the roster.

## Blast

A *Blast* grenade affects every model within its radius of the landing point that has line of sight to the point. Walls and closed doors block blast.

**Frag.** Roll the damage dice once. Read the result separately for each model, stepping down by each model's own Armour. A model in Heavy cover relative to the landing point steps down one extra row.

- **Confined:** if the landing point and the model are in the same compartment of 6"×6" or smaller, add 1 row.

**Flashbang.** Every model within 3" with line of sight to the point gains 2 Suppression, loses its Overwatch marker, and cannot react until the end of the round.

**Smoke.** Place a 2"-radius cloud. It blocks line of sight into, out of and through it until the end of the next round. Smoke has no effect in vacuum.

## Breaching charges

Placing a breaching charge on a door, hatch or thin bulkhead within 1" is an **Interact**; enemies who can see the charge may react. The charge can be **detonated** by the model that placed it, as an action, in the same activation or a later one, from anywhere.

When it detonates:

- The door, hatch or a 2" section of bulkhead is **destroyed**, leaving an opening.
- Models within **1"** of the charge, on either side, take **2d6** damage (roll once, step down by each model's Armour).
- Models within **2"** on the far side gain **2 Suppression** and cannot react until the end of the round.
- It counts as a **breach** for noise.

A charge placed on **hull** causes decompression (chapter 8).

## The breach drill

Stack beside the door, place the charge, detonate, throw a flashbang through the opening, then enter. Each step is one action. Two models working together can do it within a single round, before the defenders recover.
