# 12. Land battles

Everything in chapters 1 to 10 works on land: the same tests, line of sight, cover, range bands, reactions, Suppression, morale, close combat, grenades and the solo opponent. This chapter adds what open country and towns need: terrain types, height, conditions, noise in the open, and a way to set up a table.

Aboard ship, use chapter 11 instead.

## Terrain

Every terrain piece is one of these types. Agree before play which type each piece is.

<!-- table:terrain.types -->
| Terrain | Cover | Movement | Line of sight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Open ground** | None. Find Cover works here (chapter 4). | Normal | Clear. |
| **Scrub, crops, light woods** | Light, for a model inside it. | Normal | Models see into and out of it, but not through more than 3" of it. |
| **Dense woods, jungle** | Light, for a model inside it; Heavy if it is also behind a trunk or rock (true line of sight). | Difficult (each inch costs 2") | Models see into and out of it, but not through more than 1" of it. |
| **Rubble, scrap, ruins** | Light, for a model inside it; Heavy behind standing walls. | Difficult (each inch costs 2") | Use true line of sight; standing walls block it. |
| **Low wall, sandbags, barricade, fallen tree** | Heavy, for a model within 1" behind it. | Obstacle (+2" to cross) | Does not block line of sight. |
| **Hedge, fence, crop row** | Light, for a model within 1" behind it. | Obstacle (+2" to cross) | A hedge blocks line of sight, except to and from models within 1" of it. A fence does not. |

### Area terrain

Scrub, woods, rubble and water are **area terrain**: a footprint, usually 6"–9" across, with trees, debris or crops on it. A model whose base is inside the footprint is *in* it.

- Cover from area terrain applies to a model inside it, whichever way the shot comes from.
- A model sees into and out of scrub and woods, but not *through* more than the distance shown. The model's own position counts: a model 2" inside dense woods cannot see out.
- Move the trees or debris aside as models move through; the footprint is what counts.

### Buildings

Buildings are small ship interiors with windows.

- **Walls** block movement and line of sight. Ordinary walls are thin, like bulkheads: a breaching charge destroys a section (chapter 6). Bunker walls cannot be breached.
- **Doors** follow chapter 3: open, closed or locked, the door sequence, and crack and throw.
- **Windows:** models see and shoot through them. A model at a window has Heavy cover. Climbing through one is an obstacle (+2").
- **Floors:** each storey is one level (3"). Stairs and ladders link them (see Height). A flat roof with a parapet gives Heavy cover to a model behind it.
- **Grenades** thrown into a room 6" × 6" or smaller get the confined bonus, as aboard ship.

### Difficult ground and obstacles

- **Difficult ground:** each inch moved in it costs 2". A Move that starts in the open and ends in woods pays double only for the part in the woods.
- **Obstacles:** crossing a low wall, hedge or fence, getting into or out of a crater or trench, or climbing through a window costs 2" extra (chapter 3).

## Height

<!-- table:terrain.height -->
- Each storey, platform or level is 3" high. Climbing stairs or a ramp costs the vertical distance as well as the horizontal; a ladder costs double the vertical distance.
- Height advantage: a shooter a full level or more above its target ignores Light cover from linear terrain, and treats Heavy cover from linear terrain as Light.
- Jumping down up to 3" is free. A model that jumps or falls further tests 2d6 + Physique, 8+; on a fail, roll 2d6 on the wound table (2d8 if the drop was more than 6"), stepped down by armour as normal.
<!-- /table -->

## Conditions

Roll once at the start of a land battle, unless the mission sets the conditions.

<!-- table:terrain.conditions -->
| d6 | Conditions | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Clear** | No effect. |
| 4 | **Rain or wind** | −1 to hit beyond 18". −1 to Throw tests. |
| 5 | **Fog** | Models are seen only within 18" (12" in a dust storm in the desert). |
| 6 | **Night** | Darkness (chapter 8): models are seen only within 12". |
<!-- /table -->

## Noise in the open

Sound carries further in the open. On land, **Loud** noise (chapter 10) is heard by enemy elements with a model within **24"**, not 12". **Very loud** noise is heard by every element on the board, as aboard ship. Inside a building, use 12" for noise that does not leave it (closed doors and no open windows).

## The solo opponent on land

The behavior lists in chapter 10 use three terms (area, approach and exit) that work aboard ship and on land. On land:

- A model's **area** is the building, terrain piece or stretch of open ground it is in.
- An **approach** is a door or window of its building, or the side of its cover facing the marker.
- A Sentry "covering the approach" sits in cover facing the marker's direction, on Overwatch.
- Aggressive models flank: they prefer a route that keeps terrain between them and the player models they can see.
- An enemy model's **exit** is its board edge, unless the mission gives it another.

## Setting up a land table

1. **Environment.** The mission or campaign names it. If not, roll:

| d6 | Environment |
|---|---|
| 1 | Urban |
| 2 | Industrial |
| 3 | Woodland |
| 4 | Jungle |
| 5 | Desert, badlands |
| 6 | Farmland |
<!-- /table -->

   Fortified positions are set by missions only.

2. **Board.** Pick the size for the forces:

<!-- table:terrain.boards -->
| Force size | Board | 12" sectors |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 8 a side | 36" × 36" | 3 × 3 |
| 9 to 16 a side | 48" × 48" | 4 × 4 |
| Larger, or long advances | 60" × 36" (the dry-erase map) | 5 × 3 |
<!-- /table -->

3. **Sectors.** Divide the board into 12" squares. Roll once for each sector on the environment's column and place what it shows near the middle of the sector. You choose which way each piece faces.

<!-- table:terrain.environments -->
| d6 | Urban | Industrial | Woodland | Jungle | Desert, badlands | Farmland | Fortified position |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Street: one wreck or vehicle (solid). | Yard: two containers (solid). | Open ground. | Scrub, 6" × 6". | Open ground. | Open field. | Trench across the sector, with a firing step. |
| 2 | Rubble, 6" × 6", with a standing wall. | Tanks or pipework (solid), about 6" across. | Scrub, 6" × 6". | Dense jungle (woods), 9" × 9". | Open ground with a crater. | Crops (scrub), 9" × 9". | Sandbag line (low wall). |
| 3 | Small building, 6" × 6", one storey with a flat roof. | Warehouse, about 6" × 9", one tall storey with a gantry inside. | Woods, 6" × 9". | Dense jungle (woods), 6" × 6", and a fallen tree (low wall). | Rocks: two pieces (solid). | Hedge or stone wall (low wall) across the sector. | Bunker, about 6" × 6", with firing slits. |
| 4 | Large building, about 6" × 9", two storeys. | Scrap heap (rubble), 6" × 6". | Woods, 6" × 6", and a fallen tree (low wall). | Stream across the sector (shallow water), with scrub on its banks. | Dune or ridge (hill) across the sector. | Farm building, 6" × 6", two storeys. | Crater field: two craters. |
| 5 | Ruined building: walls without a roof, rubble inside. | Crates and low walls (low wall), two pieces. | Hedge or fence line across the sector. | Overgrown ruins: rubble with a standing wall. | Scrub, 6" × 6". | Copse (woods), 6" × 6". | Wire across the sector. |
| 6 | Barricade (low wall) across the sector, and a wreck. | Raised platform or gantry (hill, one level) with ladders. | Rocky outcrop: a hill with two rocks (solid) on it. | Rocky rise: a hill with a rock (solid). | Outpost: a small building and a low wall. | Vehicles or machinery: two pieces (solid). | Rubble, 6" × 6". |
| Fill | Rubble, 6" × 6". | Two containers (solid). | Scrub, 6" × 6". | Scrub, 6" × 6". | Two rocks (solid). | Hedge line across the sector. | Crater. |
<!-- /table -->

4. **Density check.** Look along each row and column of sectors, edge to edge. If three sectors in a line have no cover at all (open ground or a field), change the middle one to the environment's **Fill**. A model should always be able to reach cover with one or two Moves.

5. **Conditions.** Roll on the conditions table.

6. **Deployment.** Unless the mission says otherwise, the player deploys within 6" of one edge, chosen by a die roll, and the enemy in the sectors farthest from it. The enemy's exit is its own edge.

A 36" × 36" table takes nine sector rolls and a conditions roll, and should be on the table in under ten minutes.

## Example

A woodland board, 36" × 36": nine sectors. By rows from the player's edge, the rolls are 1, 5, 2 / 1, 1, 1 / 6, 4, 2.

- **Row 1:** open ground; a hedge line; scrub.
- **Row 2:** open; open; open.
- **Row 3:** a rocky outcrop; woods with a fallen tree; scrub.

**Density check.** Row 2 is three sectors in a line with no cover, so its middle sector becomes the woodland fill: scrub, 6" × 6". Every other row and column already has cover in it.

**Conditions.** The roll is 5: haze, so models are seen only within 18".

**Deployment.** The player rolls for an edge, deploys within 6" of it, and the enemy sets up in row 3. Ten rolls in all.
