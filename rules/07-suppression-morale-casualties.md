# 7. Suppression, morale and casualties

## Suppression

Each Suppression marker gives **−1 to all of that model's rolls**. A model can have at most **5**.

At **3 or more**, the model is **Pinned**. A Pinned model may only:

- Rally
- Find Cover
- Move, if it ends in cover or out of enemy line of sight
- Shoot, but only if it is in cover

A Pinned model may only react with Find Cover. A model with 2 or more Suppression loses its Overwatch marker.

**Recovery:** at the end of each round, a model that was not hit that round and is in cover removes 1 Suppression.

## Rally

**Rally** removes 1 Suppression from the model itself or from a friendly model within 6" in line of sight. It removes **2** if the rallying model is a *Leader*, or if a Leader is within 6" and in line of sight of the model being rallied.

## Casualty shock

When a friendly model within 6" goes Down or Dead, a model gains **1 Suppression**, or **2** if the casualty was its Leader.

## Breaking

When half of a fireteam (rounded up) is Down, Dead, captured or fled, each remaining model of that fireteam takes a **breaking test** at the start of its activation: 2d6 + Nerve, +1 if its Leader is within 6". The usual test modifiers apply (−1 per Suppression, −1 if Wounded).

- **8+:** it fights on this activation.
- **Fail:** it spends both actions Moving away from the enemy, toward its entry point or the nearest cover out of sight. These are ordinary Moves: they trigger reactions, and a Pinned model must still end in cover or out of sight.

Clarifications:

- **Every activation.** A model tests at the start of every activation while its fireteam is at half strength or worse. Passing once does not make it immune.
- **Who counts.** A character that joined a fireteam (chapter 9), or any model attached to it, counts as part of it. A character acting alone never tests. Each enemy element is a fireteam. *Automated* models never test.
- **Recovered models count again.** A model that gets back up is no longer a casualty, so the fireteam may climb back above half strength and stop testing.
- **Fleeing.** A breaking model that leaves the board through its side's entry point has **fled**. It takes no further part in the mission, but it is not a casualty.

## Wounds

| State | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Wounded** | Rows 4–5 add a **Wounded marker**. A model with any Wounded markers has −1 to all rolls: a flat −1, however many markers it has. When it has more Wounded markers than its **Wounds** attribute, it goes Down. Most models have Wounds 1, so a second Wounded result puts them Down. |
| **Down** (row 6) | Lies prone, takes no actions, counts as out of the fight. It may recover. |
| **Bleeding** (row 7) | Down, and dies at the end of the third round after it fell unless a Medic action gets 8+. |
| **Critical** (row 8) | Down, and dies at the end of the next round unless a Medic action gets 10+. |
| **Dead** (rows 9–10) | Removed. At row 10, friendly models within 1" gain 1 Suppression. |

When a model goes Down, Bleeding or Critical, remove its Suppression markers and Overwatch marker.

### Down recovery

At the end of each round, each **Down** model (not Bleeding or Critical) tests 2d6 + Physique. Wounded markers do not modify this test, and a Down model has no Suppression, so it is a plain 2d6 + Physique.

- **8+:** it gets up with **3 Suppression** (Pinned) and its Wounded markers set to its Wounds attribute (any extra are removed), so it has −1 to all rolls and one more Wounded result puts it Down again.
- **Fail:** it stays Down.
- **Under guard:** a Down model in base contact with a standing enemy model does not test.

### Capture

If the mission allows prisoners, a model in base contact with an enemy that is Down, Bleeding or Critical may **Interact** to capture it. A captured model is out of the mission; mark it and leave it in place until it is moved (missions say how prisoners are moved). Bleeding and Critical clocks keep running on captured models.

### Medic action

A model in base contact with a Down, Bleeding or Critical friend may use the **Medic** action: test 2d6 + Tech (+2 if it has the *Medic* skill).

| Patient | Result |
|---|---|
| **Down** | 8+: the patient gets up as for Down recovery. A model with the *Medic* skill succeeds automatically. |
| **Bleeding** | 8+: the bleeding stops; the patient becomes Down and may recover normally. |
| **Critical** | 10+: the patient is stabilised. It stays Down for the rest of the mission but will not die. |

Casualties carry into the campaign: a model that went Down during the mission and lived rolls for injury afterwards, and a model left behind Down is captured (chapter 14).
