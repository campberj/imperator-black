# 1. Core concepts

## What you need

- **Models:** one per soldier, on 25–32 mm bases.
- **Dice:** two d6 in one colour (the **hit dice**), and a pair each of d4, d6, d8, d10 and d12 in a second colour (the **damage dice**).
- **Tokens:** Suppression, Wounded, Down, Overwatch, Activated, and numbered contact chips.
- **Terrain:** for boarding actions, 6"×6", 6"×3" and 3"×3" tiles with walls and doors.
- **A ruler** measuring in inches.

**Scale:** 1" = 2 m. A 6" tile is a 12 m compartment; a 3"-wide corridor is 6 m across.

## Model profile

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Quality (Q)** | Training and experience, 0–3. Added to hit rolls and tests. |
| **Armour** | 0–3. Steps wound results down (chapter 4). |
| **Move** | 6", or 5" in Heavy armour. |
| **Weapons** | One ranged weapon, a melee weapon, and grenades or gear as the roster lists. |
| **Keywords** | Traits such as *Leader*, *Medic*, *Void-trained*. |

## Tests

A **test** is 2d6 + Q, minus 1 per Suppression marker and 1 if Wounded, against a target number. The target is **8** unless a rule says otherwise. A natural 2 always fails.

## Measuring

Measure in inches, base edge to base edge. You may measure at any time.

## Line of sight

Use true line of sight from the model's eye. A model can see a target if any part of the target's body (not weapon or antenna) is visible. Walls and closed doors block line of sight. Other models do not block it, but they give the target Light cover.

## Rounding and modifiers

Modifiers are cumulative. Round fractions of inches down. When a rule says "half", round down.

## Order of precedence

When a specific rule conflicts with a general one, the specific rule wins. When two rules conflict and neither is more specific, the one less favourable to the activating player applies.
