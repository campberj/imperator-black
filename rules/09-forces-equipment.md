# 9. Forces and equipment

## Reading a profile

| Entry | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Green, Regular, Veteran or Elite. A label only (chapter 1). |
| **MOV SHT FGT PHY NRV TEC** | Move, Shoot, Fight, Physique, Nerve, Tech (chapter 1). A dash means never used. |
| **W** | Wounds: Wounded markers the model can carry before it goes Down. |
| **SZ** | Size: 1 small drone, 2 human, 3 large. |
| **Armour** | Armour type, with its value in brackets (chapter 4). |
| **Weapon** | Ranged weapon. Alternatives after a slash may be swapped in when the roster is built. |
| **Kit** | Melee weapon, grenades (two per model), and gear. "Option" gear is chosen per model when the roster is built. |
| **Skills** | Model skills, with levels where they have them (table below). Gear can also grant skills. |

Every model carries a melee weapon. A model with no melee weapon listed (only *Automated* models) cannot start close combat and always loses an exchange it is forced into.

### Statting a new miniature

Pick the rank that fits, then set the main attributes near that rank's typical value and adjust for the model's job: a marksman's Shoot goes up, a heavy gunner's Physique goes up and its Move down. Keep most values within one point of the typical value.

<!-- table:core.ranks -->
| Rank | Typical attributes | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Green | 0-1 | New to the job: crew, conscripts, fresh hires. |
| Regular | 1 | Trained and steady: most soldiers and professional security. |
| Veteran | 2 | Experienced: team leaders, specialists, seasoned contractors. |
| Elite | 3 | The best there is: special operators, intelligence handlers. |
<!-- /table -->

## Building a force

Forces are built from **teams**, like a table of organisation, not from a points list.

- A **team** has fixed role slots. Fill each slot with its troop type and choose any weapon and gear options.
- The proof-of-concept campaign starts with the **Boarding element**: one VAT fireteam plus one attached Pathfinder or Marksman, six models in all. One VAT Assaulter usually carries a breaching charge and the other a trauma kit.
- How the force grows, through requisition, replacements, promotions and new teams, is part of the campaign (Phase 7).

## Galactic Republic

Profiles for Star Wars: Legion Galactic Republic miniatures: clone troopers, specialists and commandos, led by clone officers and Jedi. They use the Shoot scale in chapter 1: clone troopers are professionals (2); ARC troopers, clone commandos and Commander Cody are elite (3).

**Jedi** fight with trained abilities, not Force powers: *Deflect*, *Leap*, *Jedi discipline* and *Twin blades* (see Skills). They carry no ranged weapon, so they cannot Shoot; a lightsaber is a melee weapon with AP 2. A Jedi's danger is close combat, and Deflect lets it close the distance under fire.

Vehicles (the AT-RT and the infantry support platform) wait for the vehicle rules, after the proof of concept.

## Near Present forces

Profiles for Echelon Software's *Near Present* miniatures: two private military contractors fighting over the Marine Contingency Platforms along the Paracletus storm wall in 2046. Each fields a **Multi-Function Response Team**: nine warfighters and their Collaborative Combat Troops (CCT), autonomous combat robots.

They use the Shoot scale in chapter 1. The line troops are professionals (2); the Anchorage Prime and the Hankou Pioneer are Shoot 3, and the Prime and the Team Leader are Veterans and Leaders. Their hyper-enabler helmets give every warfighter low-light optics.

**Conversion notes.** Move is the *Near Present* movement range less 2". *For Effect* (two attack rolls spread over 3") becomes an auto rifle (Auto 2); *Breacher* a breaching charge; *Grenadier Front* a grenade launcher; *Cognitive Jammer* a jammer; Hankou's *Laser Detector* the skill of that name. A CCT is Automated, with its load-out table as weapon and gear options: the Hound is heavy and tough (Size 3, Wounds 3), the ZQ44 light and attritable. Drones, loitering munitions, launch tubes and the airspace phase (*Pusher*, ISR drones, *Flux Grenade*, C-RAM) wait for the air and void layer; see [docs/design/air-and-void.md](../docs/design/air-and-void.md).
